### 75–450 (2.5–15s) — Name and define once
**On-screen:**
> Piaget: children don't think "less" than adults. They think **differently** — through 4 stages, each a different operating system.

**VO:** `Piaget ke mutabik, bachche adults se kam nahi sochte — woh alag tarah se sochte hain. Chaar stages hain, aur har stage ek naya "operating system" hai.`

### 450–1050 (15–35s) — Animated diagram: ascending stair, 4 steps
**On-screen (each step labels in on-screen text as VO reaches it):**
1. **Sensorimotor** (0–2 yrs) — object permanence
2. **Pre-operational** (2–7 yrs) — centration, egocentrism, no conservation
3. **Concrete Operational** (7–11 yrs) — conservation, reversibility, seriation
4. **Formal Operational** (11+ yrs) — abstract, hypothetical reasoning

**VO:** `Pehla — sensorimotor, do saal tak. Yahaan object permanence banta hai. Doosra — pre-operational, do se saat saal. Yahaan bachcha sirf ek cheez par focus karta hai, isliye conservation fail karta hai. Teesra — concrete operational, saat se gyarah saal. Ab bachcha ek saath do cheezein dekh sakta hai — conservation aa jaata hai, reversibility bhi. Chautha — formal operational, gyarah ke baad. Ab bachcha bina kisi cheez ko chhue, sirf sochkar hypothetical sawaal solve kar sakta hai.`
