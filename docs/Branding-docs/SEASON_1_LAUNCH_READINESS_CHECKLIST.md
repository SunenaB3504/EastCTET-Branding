# EasyCTET Season 1 Launch Readiness Checklist

**Target:** Focused CTET Paper 1 launch before the 9 October 2026 exam

**Current scope:** CDP, English, EVS, Hindi, and Mathematics

**Launch posture:** Self-funded, founder-led validation launch. Prioritise reliability, trust, first customers, and learning. Defer broad expansion until the next season.

## How to Use This Checklist

Mark an item complete only when it has been tested or evidenced. Record links, screenshots, test results, or decisions beside important items. Items marked **Launch blocker** must be complete before public promotion.

## 1. Product Scope and Brand Promise

- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Confirm the app is presented as CTET Paper 1 only.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Confirm the visible subjects are CDP, English, EVS, Hindi, and Mathematics.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Remove Paper 2, Marathi, Science, and Social Studies from current launch copy.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Use the approved tagline: `Serious CTET Paper 1 prep, simplified.`
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Add the independent, non-affiliation statement where required.
- [ ] Confirm no page, screenshot, or message promises qualification, a score, or first-attempt success.
- [ ] Confirm the free tier and Pro Pass scope are identical across the app, Play Store, website, and messages.

## 2. App Functional Readiness

- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Production build installs successfully on the target Android version.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** App opens and completes onboarding without an account requirement, if that remains the product decision.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Free content is accessible without payment.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Paid content is correctly locked and unlocked after a successful purchase.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Purchase restoration works after reinstall or device change.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Questions, options, answer keys, and explanations display correctly.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Timer, navigation, answer selection, flagging, submission, and score calculation work correctly.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Progress survives app close, interruption, and relaunch.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Mistake history and progress tracking do not lose or corrupt records.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Core practice remains usable without internet after installation.
- [ ] Confirm downloaded audio works offline, if audio is included in the launch build.
- [ ] Confirm the app behaves acceptably on at least one lower-spec Android phone.
- [ ] Confirm Android back navigation does not lose an active test accidentally.
- [ ] Confirm reset-local-data behaviour works and is clearly labelled.

## 3. Content Quality and Coverage

- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Validate every question ID is unique.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Validate every question has the expected number of options.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Validate every correct answer points to an existing option.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Validate every question has a non-empty explanation.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Review answer keys and explanations for factual accuracy.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Check for duplicate or near-duplicate questions.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Check English and Hindi text for grammar, spelling, and rendering issues.
- [ ] Confirm topic tags map to the five current Paper 1 subjects.
- [ ] Confirm content is original and does not present internal style references as official past-paper attribution.
- [ ] Record the content review date and reviewer name for each subject.
- [ ] Prepare a correction process for users who report a questionable answer or explanation.

## 4. Privacy, Legal, and Trust

- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Publish the Privacy Policy at a stable HTTPS URL.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Publish the Terms of Service at a stable HTTPS URL.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Replace generic operator wording with the real legal name or business name.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Add the real support email and effective date.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Confirm the legal documents match actual app behaviour.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Complete the Google Play Data Safety form truthfully.
- [ ] Confirm whether Google Play Billing, install referrer, support email, or diagnostics change the privacy disclosures.
- [ ] Confirm no analytics, ad trackers, or unapproved SDKs are included.
- [ ] Confirm the government non-affiliation disclaimer is visible in the app or store materials.
- [ ] Obtain a qualified legal review if budget and timing permit, especially for billing, refunds, privacy, and referral terms.

## 5. Payments and Commercial Readiness

- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Confirm the legal seller or proprietor identity used for Play Console and payment setup.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Configure the final Paper 1 product/SKU in Google Play Console.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Confirm final price, tax display, and purchase wording.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Test successful purchase using a licence tester account.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Test cancelled, declined, pending, and restored purchases.
- [ ] Confirm the app does not describe a subscription if the product is a one-time purchase.
- [ ] Confirm refund instructions point users to the correct Google Play process.
- [ ] Defer complex referral rewards until the basic purchase and support flow is reliable.

## 6. Google Play Release Readiness

- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Complete developer account verification.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Complete app content, target audience, content rating, and data safety declarations.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Upload app icon, feature graphic, screenshots, short description, and full description.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Confirm store copy says Paper 1 and lists only the five current subjects.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Add the privacy-policy URL to the store listing.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Upload the production build to internal or closed testing.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Install the store build from Google Play, not only a development APK.
- [ ] Complete a closed test with a small group of trusted users.
- [ ] Record store-review or release issues and their resolution.

## 7. Support and Operations

- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Confirm `support@easyctet.in` or the final support address receives mail.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Prepare responses for installation, payment, content correction, offline use, and data reset.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Define a realistic support response window around the full-time job.
- [ ] Create a private issue log with date, user problem, severity, status, and resolution.
- [ ] Create a rollback or hotfix decision process for serious production issues.
- [ ] Keep a private record of purchases, refunds, support cases, and content corrections without collecting unnecessary personal data.
- [ ] Prepare a simple incident message in case the app or payment service is temporarily unavailable.

## 8. Launch Communications

- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Prepare one personal-network launch message.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Prepare one teacher-community message.
- [ ] **Launch blocker:** Prepare one public social post.
- [ ] Prepare seven useful daily questions for the first week.
- [ ] Prepare two short concept posts or videos for launch week.
- [ ] Prepare one honest founder message explaining that this is a focused first release.
- [ ] Ensure every message says Paper 1 and names the five subjects.
- [ ] Ensure every promotional message includes one clear action.
- [ ] Avoid guaranteed results, fear-based urgency, spam, and unapproved community promotion.
- [ ] Do not pay for ads or influencer campaigns until organic usage shows repeat demand.

## 9. Soft Launch Gate

Do not begin broad promotion until these checks pass with trusted users:

- [ ] A new user understands the app's Paper 1 scope without explanation.
- [ ] A user can start free practice within a few minutes.
- [ ] A user can complete a test and understand the result.
- [ ] A user can find the explanation for an incorrect answer.
- [ ] A user can use core practice after disabling internet.
- [ ] A test interruption does not destroy progress.
- [ ] A tester can complete or simulate the purchase flow.
- [ ] Support receives and answers a test request.
- [ ] No critical crash, data loss, incorrect answer key, or payment entitlement issue remains open.

## 10. First-Season Measurement

Record these in a private spreadsheet without adding unnecessary tracking to the app:

- [ ] Website launch-update requests
- [ ] Play Store listing visits, where available
- [ ] Installs
- [ ] First quiz starts
- [ ] First quiz completions
- [ ] Repeat practice within 7 days
- [ ] Free-to-paid conversions
- [ ] Refunds and payment failures
- [ ] Support issues by category
- [ ] Reported content corrections
- [ ] User comments and feature requests

Review at:

- [ ] 24 hours after soft launch
- [ ] 7 days after public launch
- [ ] Immediately after the 9 October exam period

## 11. Explicitly Defer to Next Season

Do not allow these items to delay the first reliable release unless they are already complete and low-risk:

- [ ] Paper 2 content
- [ ] Marathi, Science, or Social Studies expansion
- [ ] Paid advertising
- [ ] Broad influencer campaigns
- [ ] Complex referral leaderboards
- [ ] Major redesigns
- [ ] Account creation and social login
- [ ] Large feature additions without evidence from Season 1 users

## Final Go / No-Go Decision

**Go only if:**

- [ ] The production app is stable.
- [ ] The Paper 1 content is accurate and clearly scoped.
- [ ] Payment and entitlement behaviour is tested.
- [ ] Privacy, Terms, and support details are live and truthful.
- [ ] A small group of users has completed the soft-launch flow.
- [ ] The founder can support the release without compromising the full-time job.
