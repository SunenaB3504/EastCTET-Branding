Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EasyCTET ("we," "us," "our," "the App") handles information in connection with the EasyCTET mobile application and the easyctet.in website (together, "the Service"). We built EasyCTET around one core principle: your study habits are your business, not ours.

1. Our Data Philosophy

EasyCTET is designed to work 100% offline, and the App collects zero telemetry. In practical terms:

  • We do not track which questions you answer, how long you study, or your weak topics.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, ad-tracking libraries, or behavioral profiling tools inside the App.
  • Your quiz scores, progress, and study history are stored only on your own device and never transmitted to us.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to your quiz answers or scores, your device's location, your contacts, photos or files, advertising identifiers, or any in-app usage analytics.

3. Information You May Choose to Provide

  • Referral Program: if you refer a friend, we may ask for a way to share your referral code — we do not access your contact list to do this.
  • Pro Pass Purchases: processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details — Google handles that under its own privacy policy.
  • Contacting Us: if you email us, we'll have your email address and message content, used only to respond to you.

4. Children's Privacy

EasyCTET is designed for adult CTET aspirants preparing to become teachers. It is not directed at or intended for use by children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Data Security

Because the App does not transmit your study data anywhere, there is nothing for us to secure on our end — your information stays on your device. Any limited data shared directly with us (such as a support email) is handled with reasonable care and never sold or shared for advertising.

6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service involved is Google Play Billing, used solely to process Pro Pass purchases, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

7. Your Rights

Since we hold minimal to no personal data about you, there is generally nothing to access, correct, or delete beyond direct correspondence, which you may ask us to delete at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the date above when we do. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy: support@easyctet.in

This policy reflects EasyCTET's actual data practices as of the date above. It has been drafted to align with general principles under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Google Play's Data Safety requirements — but is a starting template, not a substitute for review by a qualified legal professional before publishing.
