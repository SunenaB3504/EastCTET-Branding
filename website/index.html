CDP

Piaget's Stages of Thinking

Children don't just "know more" as they grow — they think in fundamentally different ways at different ages. A recurring exam favorite is spotting why a young child gets a simple logic puzzle wrong.

Meera, age 4, watches her teacher pour the same amount of juice from a short, wide cup into a tall, narrow one. She insists the tall cup now has "more" juice. This shows Meera has not yet developed:
A) Object permanence   B) Conservation   C) Scaffolding   D) Assimilation
Show Answer
B — Conservation. Meera is focusing only on the height of the liquid (centration) and hasn't yet grasped that quantity stays the same despite a change in appearance.

EVS

Teaching EVS the Right Way

EVS is meant to be taught through observation and hands-on activity, not memorized definitions.

A teacher plans a nature walk for her Class 3 students. What is the primary pedagogical value of this activity?
A) Memorizing more plant names   B) Building observation and inquiry skills   C) A break from routine   D) Covering the syllabus faster
Show Answer
B. Field-based activities in EVS are valued for developing observation and inquiry, not rote memorization.

English — Language I

Teaching Methods, Side by Side

Nearly every question here is really asking: can you tell one teaching method apart from a similar-sounding one?

A teacher gives her students simple English conversation prompts and lets them absorb grammar naturally through context, without ever stating a grammar rule directly. Which method is this?
A) Grammar-Translation Method   B) Direct Method   C) Audio-Lingual Method   D) Bilingual Method
Show Answer
B — Direct Method. It relies on exposure and context in the target language, without explicit rule-teaching or translation.

Device Changes, Purchases & Data

What happens if I change my phone or reinstall EasyCTET?+
Your Pro Pass is linked to your Google Play account, not the physical phone. Install EasyCTET on your new device using the same Google account, then go to Settings > Restore Purchases — your pass activates immediately at no extra charge. Because EasyCTET keeps everything on-device rather than on a central server, your test scores and mistake ledger stay on your old phone and start fresh on the new one.
How do I reset my test scores to start revision from scratch?+

Will updating the app from the Google Play Store erase my progress?+

Self-Troubleshooting & Offline

What is "1-Tap Quick Repair" and when should I use it?+
If an exam session ever freezes or questions load oddly, you don't need to reinstall or email support. Go to Settings > 1-Tap Quick Repair — it re-verifies the question vault and clears stale session cache in under 1 second, without erasing your past scores.
What do the error codes (e.g. L1-DATA-002, L2-TTS-001) mean?+
EasyCTET uses on-device diagnostic codes so you always know what's happening, even offline: L0-OK-000 means everything is healthy; L1-DATA-002 is a session data glitch, fixed by 1-Tap Quick Repair; L2-TTS-001 means a device audio/speech issue — check Text-to-Speech is enabled in Android Settings; L3-BILL-001 means Google Play couldn't be reached — check your connection and retry. You can also tap "Draft Diagnostic Email" in Settings to send a pre-filled report to support.
Why is the Audio Masterclass not playing sound?+
EasyCTET uses your Android device's built-in Text-to-Speech engine so audio works fully offline without using mobile data. If you hear nothing, check your media volume, and make sure Text-to-Speech output (Google Speech Services or your phone's built-in TTS) is enabled under Android Settings > System > Languages & Input.
Does the app really work with no internet connection?+
Yes. Once installed, every question, study guide, and audio masterclass works fully offline — no signal needed to practice. The only moment an internet connection is used is a brief handshake with Google Play when you purchase or tap "Restore Purchases."
What does "Zero Telemetry" actually mean for my privacy?+
We don't collect analytics on how you use the app, don't use ad trackers, and don't sell or share your data — there's no central server logging your test attempts. You never need to create an account or provide an email or phone number. Your study progress stays on your own device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
